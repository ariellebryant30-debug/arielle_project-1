James Talarico Fights to Take Power Back for Working People

By Arielle Bryant

October 21, 2026

After winning Texas’s U.S. Senate Democratic Primary by a narrow margin against incumbent Jasmine Crockett, Talarico is fighting to win the election. He has made major promises to fight “top to bottom,” not “left to right,” and is focusing on corruption, labor, health care, among other pressing issues.

Corruption & Democracy

Talarico does not accept corporate PAC donations. He has fought against redistricting maps. In the Senate, he plans to introduce an anti-corruption package to ban super PACs and corporate PACs, ban partisan gerrymandering, and increase transparency and accountability in all branches of government. He also plans to an partisan gerrymandering nationwide, ban Congressional stock trading, institute term limits on Members of Congress, ban presidential pardons, and implement term limits and a binding code of conduct for U.S. Supreme Court justices, including mandating rules for recusals, financial disclosures, and limitations on accepting gifts.

Health care

In the Senate, Talarico plans to pass legislation to allow every American — regardless of their age — to join Medicare. He also plans to restore access to reproductive health care in Texas by codifying Roe v. Wade. He has promised to save rural health care by prioritizing recruiting and retaining rural health care workers.

Labor & Business

Talarico’s main promise is to raise the federal minimum wage to $15 an hour. Notably, he also promises to pass paid family leave legislation that would allow Americans to take time off work to bond with a new child or care for a seriously ill family member.

Energy & Environment

Following serious grid failures in Texas, Talarico’s energy promises prioritize weatherproofing the grid. He also plans to lower electric bills, grow the economy, and combat climate change.
